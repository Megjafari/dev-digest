**CNCF**

Volcano v1.16 has been released with new queue management capabilities relevant to Kubernetes workload scheduling. The update introduces NamespaceQueue, allowing tenants to organize hierarchical queues within their own namespaces for better quota management, and a fairshare plugin for more equitable resource distribution across workloads. These features may be useful if you are running batch jobs or multi-tenant workloads on Kubernetes clusters alongside your containerized .NET applications. The release also expands the HyperNode ecosystem for broader cluster resource utilization.
