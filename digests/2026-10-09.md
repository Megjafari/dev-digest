### War and conflicts

Saudi Arabia has reopened Riyadh airport following a Houthi attack that killed three people, according to Al Jazeera and the BBC. Multiple sources report that the Houthis claimed responsibility for the strikes, which mark an escalation in the conflict between the Iran-backed group and Saudi Arabia. According to Al Jazeera, Saudi Arabia has experienced several Houthi attacks in recent days with airports appearing to be the primary target. The BBC describes the attacks as part of broader escalating fighting between the Iran-backed Houthis and Saudi Arabia, which backs Yemen's government. Al Jazeera also reports on speculation in US media about potential military action against Iran, though it notes the US president has ruled out strikes before upcoming elections.

### AI worldwide

OpenAI has reaffirmed its decision to terminate three AI safety researchers after concluding they committed a significant breach of trust, according to a statement posted Friday. The company's firm stance on the dismissals signals tensions within the organization over safety priorities. Meanwhile, Anthropic is taking a different approach by launching a free security scanning service for open-source projects, offering periodic vulnerability assessments powered by its strongest AI models at no cost. On the regulatory front, California's State Athletic Commission has sent a cease-and-desist letter to a startup that hosted a human versus robot cage match last month, marking an effort to establish oversight of such events. Separately, researchers and commentators are raising concerns about over-reliance on AI systems' ability to refuse harmful requests, noting that this expectation may not match the actual capabilities of current models.
