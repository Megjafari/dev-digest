### AI worldwide

Python 3.15 has been released, marking another step in the language's evolution as maintainers phase out older versions and developers working on open source software gain new capabilities to leverage. In the broader AI landscape, significant corporate movements are reshaping the sector: Character.AI, an early generative AI startup that built various chatbots for users, has filed for bankruptcy and is selling assets to Disney after facing mounting pressures. Apple has launched HomeView, its first smart display designed for the home, featuring a vibrant screen mounted on a speaker base for video calls and home control. Meta is intensifying its AI efforts by releasing new models through its revamped Superintelligence Labs division, aiming to compete with leading industry models. Meanwhile, AI labs are acquiring data from defunct startups to train their models, a trend that highlights how companies are sourcing training material in an increasingly consolidated market. Questions persist about whether open AI models deliver sufficient benefits to justify their potential risks, a debate that reflects ongoing tensions in how the technology industry approaches transparency and safety.
