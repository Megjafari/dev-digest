### DevOps and software development

A significant security concern has emerged around AI assisted development practices. GitGuardian's latest findings show that developers are leaking secrets into large language models at roughly twice the normal rate, with over 1.27 million AI service leaks detected in 2025 representing an 81 percent jump from the previous year. If your team is using AI tools to generate or review code, you should audit what credentials and connection strings are being exposed in those contexts.

On the Kubernetes infrastructure side, etcd has released v3.7.0 with the long requested RangeStream feature, and there is active discussion around using node swap to handle memory pressure as AI workloads demand increasingly large memory footprints. The kubelet also now watches inode availability more closely to prevent emergency situations on worker nodes. GitHub has tightened npm trusted publishing security by requiring validation within 48 hours, which may affect your deployment pipelines if you use that feature.

### AI worldwide

Several developments highlight expanding AI capabilities and emerging challenges across different domains. Google is exploring how its "Call for Me" feature could expand beyond business calls to enable AI to contact friends and family on your behalf, raising questions about consent and communication norms. Meanwhile, a healthcare startup in Utah is now issuing AI-generated acne prescriptions after analyzing users' selfies, representing a significant step in autonomous medical decision-making. On the infrastructure side, enterprises are grappling with how to connect AI agents to internal knowledge systems that can contextualize their vast data stores into actionable insights. A new bug affecting Amazon Alexa Plus has caused some Echo speakers to repeatedly sing "lalala" for extended periods, illustrating the unpredictability of voice assistant systems. Separately, researchers continue exploring the limits and capabilities of large language models, such as their ability to compute complex mathematical operations and express results in natural language.
