### War and conflicts

Sri Lanka's former first lady Shiranthi Rajapaksa has been arrested in a corruption case, according to Al Jazeera. In maritime developments, reports indicate that tanker captains are earning substantial bonuses to transit the Strait of Hormuz amid regional tensions, with Al Jazeera reporting figures of $100,000 plus $50,000 per trip. The UK and Israel have moved to de-escalate diplomatic tensions following disputes over Israeli settlements, with the Guardian and BBC reporting that most UK diplomats will leave the East Jerusalem consulate while limited services continue. Regarding the Gaza conflict, the BBC reports that Palestinians continue to face Israeli strikes despite ceasefire discussions, with many living in tent cities as peace efforts stall. Oil prices have jumped 5 percent according to the Guardian, driven by Middle East tensions and hurricane concerns, amid reports the White House is considering military options. The BBC also reports that a wanted Syrian general is being sheltered by the US and Lebanon in exchange for information including details about an abducted American journalist.

### AI worldwide

Anthropic has released Claude Haiku 5.5, a new fast and low cost model intended to replace the previous Haiku 4.5 from nearly a year ago. OpenAI is rolling out an Intelligent UI feature in ChatGPT that fills responses with interactive visuals including pictures, charts, and buttons, launching alongside GPT-6. Microsoft held a major Windows and Surface event in San Francisco where it announced the Surface Laptop Ultra powered by Nvidia's RTX Spark Arm-based chip, and revealed plans to give its Copilot AI system access to local files and the ability to take actions across the operating system through a feature called Hybrid Intelligence. Industrial AI is entering a new phase as advances in foundation models and agentic AI enable automation of more complex tasks across industrial environments. Meanwhile, experts caution that despite robotics breakthroughs, such advances are unlikely to significantly impact daily life in the near term.
