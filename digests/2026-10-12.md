### DevOps and software development

QGIS has implemented a policy banning AI assisted contributions to its project, a decision that generated significant discussion in the developer community about the role of AI tools in open source software development. On the DevOps front, several infrastructure security topics are trending: certificate transparency logging offers a way to detect phishing attempts before they go live by monitoring when new certificates are issued, while SSH hardening with Ed25519 keys remains critical for defending Linux servers against automated brute force attacks that target public IPs within minutes of deployment. A more complex challenge emerging in enterprise systems is managing permissions in RAG implementations, where access controls and content updates operate on different timescales and baking permissions into vector indices at embedding time can create dangerous security gaps. These topics reflect ongoing tensions between development velocity, security practices, and the evolving role of automation in infrastructure management.
