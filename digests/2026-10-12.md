### War and conflicts

Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps is reportedly striking ships it alleges are part of an operation to shuttle crude oil beyond the Strait of Hormuz, according to Al Jazeera. The Houthis have launched attacks on Saudi airports that mark the deadliest assault on Saudi soil in more than a decade, prompting Saudi Arabia to suspend flights following the latest strike, as reported by both Al Jazeera and The Guardian. According to the BBC, while physical damage from these attacks can be quickly repaired, Saudi Arabia's reputation for stability is suffering significant damage. The Guardian quotes Saudi Arabia's energy minister as saying "fear has no place in the Saudi lexicon" amid the extended flight suspensions. A BBC report notes that the British consulate in East Jerusalem, established in 1838, is set to be closed by the Israeli government in retaliation for UK sanctions.

### DevOps and software development

GitHub experienced an incident affecting Actions, Pull Requests and Issues, which warrants attention if you rely on those services for your deployment pipelines and infrastructure automation. More critically, Zod version 4.6.6 has been compromised by malware, so if your backend projects depend on this validation library you should audit your dependencies immediately and update to a patched version. The incident around Zod generated significant discussion in the developer community and represents a supply chain risk worth monitoring in your .NET and Node.js toolchains. Beyond these immediate concerns, there is emerging discussion around monitoring challenges specific to AI APIs where traditional HTTP status checks prove insufficient for determining actual service health. If you are running custom services in your Kubernetes clusters, there is also fresh guidance available on writing and managing systemd services for Linux environments. Additionally, Microsoft has been pushing migration to the Graph PowerShell SDK v2 as legacy Azure identity modules reach end of life, which may affect your Azure automation workflows.
