### AI worldwide

There is significant discussion around the risks and limitations of using language models to create content, with one prominent piece arguing that using LLMs to author posts reveals intellectual shortcuts in a way that matters to readers and writers. On the practical side, developers are finding creative uses for the technology, from porting decades-old video game code to modern engines with AI assistance to building high-performance inference systems that run directly in web browsers. Meanwhile, the AI tools themselves continue to evolve, with projects like Datasette adding observability features and new infrastructure tools gaining branch support for development workflows. There remains a curious paradox in how people relate to AI: despite widespread skepticism and concern about hallucinations causing real problems like dangerous restaurant scenarios, demand for AI applications continues to grow even as users express distrust and frustration with the technology.
