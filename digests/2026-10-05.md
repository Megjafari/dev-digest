### AI worldwide

Google has released two new Gemini text-to-speech models with access to over two thousand voices and the ability to create custom voices, expanding options for developers building voice-enabled applications. Research comparing decision models like Jev against traditional approaches found that LLM-as-a-judge and conventional classifiers still perform competitively, suggesting that newer specialized models may not universally outperform established methods. Enterprise AI is shifting focus from whether predictive models can beat statistical forecasts to how these systems can act autonomously on their conclusions while maintaining reliability and control. A broader concern about artificial intelligence is whether human cognition is equipped to fully comprehend and manage the capabilities these systems are developing. The intersection of agentic AI and software development continues to attract attention from builders experimenting with coding agents and related technologies.
