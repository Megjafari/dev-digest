### War and conflicts

During Palestine's olive harvest, settlers have set fire to trees and vehicles according to Al Jazeera, which describes the incidents as part of escalating settler actions. The UN is warning that nearly one million displaced Palestinians in Gaza face flooding risks as winter approaches. China has called on Israel to stop what it describes as the forced establishment of West Bank settlements, according to BBC Middle East reporting on remarks by Chinese Foreign Minister Wang Yi to Israeli President Isaac Herzog. A Houthi missile attack on a Yemeni presidential palace killed five people according to Yemen's information minister, though the minister said the prime minister and other government officials were unhurt. Meanwhile, a new documentary called NAZA about Israeli military AI systems has become a point of contention, with The Guardian reporting on filmmaker Jonathan Glazer's criticism of what he calls Israel's retaliation against those involved in the film. President Trump has stated he rules out using nuclear weapons on Iran, though according to Al Jazeera he did not deny the United States could employ other weapons systems.

### AI worldwide

Anthropic has reported quarterly revenue nearly 50 percent higher at 16.8 billion dollars ahead of an anticipated initial public offering, according to disclosures made to investors. Meanwhile, the entertainment industry is reflecting a broader cultural moment with multiple 2026 films taking a more critical perspective on technology founders and Silicon Valley's influence on society.
